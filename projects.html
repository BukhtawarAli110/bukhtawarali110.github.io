Projects

Six Power BI projects built while retraining in data analytics. Each one has a README, the report file, the data and screenshots on GitHub, and each answers a business question with DAX, data modelling and clear dashboard design.

Global Superstore Profitability Analysis

Power BI · 2026

  • DAX
  • Star Schema
  • Data Modelling
  • Executive Dashboards
  • Three-page executive report (Overview, Customers, Supply Chain) analysing 12.64M in sales across 25,035 orders and 13 regions, built on a star schema with a Key Measures table
  • Defined net profit as profit after shipping cost, with YoY growth, return rate, repeat-customer rate and freight-drag measures
  • Found that shipping costs 10.7% of sales and cuts the profit margin from 11.6% to 0.91%
  • Showed that Furniture and three regions lose money after freight, and that 44% of customer accounts are unprofitable

Pharma Sales, Returns and Target Analysis

Power BI · 2026

  • DAX
  • Star Schema
  • Pareto Analysis
  • Time Intelligence
  • Four-page report analysing 2.72bn in sales revenue across 1.2M transactions, 54 distributors, 19 states and 11 brands
  • Dynamic Pareto chart with a dimension selector (Brand, Product Group, State, Plant) and an adjustable threshold slider
  • YTD, MTD and FTD target-vs-achievement measures (Jan to Oct 2022 sales reached 93% of target), plus a switch between quantity and rupee views of sales and returns
  • Found that five brands drive 67% of revenue, five states drive 48%, and returns stay low at 3.5% of sales

Row-Level Security and What-If Scenario Dashboard

Power BI · 2026

  • DAX
  • Row-Level Security
  • What-If Parameters
  • Field Parameters
  • One dashboard that shows different data to an Executive, a Regional Manager and Sales Staff, using a dynamic row-level security role (USERPRINCIPALNAME) on a user-to-region table, plus a DAX-driven governance banner, lock icon and dynamic titles
  • Masked margin for Level 1 users (restricted table, KPI cards and chart series), while managers and executives see the full figures for their regions
  • Built a discount what-if slider and a metric field parameter that recalculate scenario revenue and margin for each user
  • Showed that a 10% discount cuts margin pounds by 15% (65.9% to 62.1% margin), because the whole discount falls on profit

Blinkit Grocery Sales Analysis

Power BI · 2026

  • DAX
  • Power Query
  • Field Parameters
  • Interactive dashboard analysing 1.20M in sales across 8,523 items, 10 outlets and 16 product categories
  • Cleaned inconsistent product labels in Power Query and built a Key Measures table of DAX measures
  • Field-parameter metric switcher lets every chart flip between Total Sales, Average Sales, Item Count and Rating

HR Attrition Analysis

Power BI · 2026

  • DAX
  • Power Query
  • Conditional Formatting
  • Interactive dashboard analysing attrition for 1,470 employees, with KPI cards, age and gender breakdowns and a job-role satisfaction heat map
  • Education-field buttons filter every visual on the page with one click
  • Found a 16.1% overall attrition rate, with overtime workers leaving at 30.5% against 10.4% for the rest
  • Sales Representatives (39.8%) and under-25s (39.2%) are the highest-risk groups

Netflix Content Analysis

Power BI · 2026

  • DAX
  • Power Query
  • Data Modelling
  • Three-page report analysing 5,806 Netflix titles and 77,000 cast and crew credits, with slicers for release year, type, country, genre and rating
  • Built KPI cards, a release-year trend with trend line, genre and maturity-rating breakdowns, and top directors and cast from the credits table
  • Found that movies make up 65% of the catalogue, the US supplies 40% of titles (India is second), Drama and Comedy lead the genre mix, and a quarter of titles are mature content
  • Flagged that 45% of titles have no age certification, the biggest data-quality gap in the dataset
